## 19. Your personal system for studying and getting things done

## 21. The 9×9 grid (Mandala Chart)
The method was created by Japanese consultant Yasuo Matsumura and became famous when Shohei Ohtani used it in high school: a goal in the centre, 8 pillars around it, and 8 steps for each pillar = 64 steps. Harvard professor Frances Frei tested it with her students; many who thrived added “character” and “reputation” to their grid. This site is the live version of the grid. Its pillars: CFA Charter, Risk Credentials, Move into Risk at Tamara, Proof of Work, Routes into PIF, Network & Reputation, Risk Toolkit, Focus & Energy. (The PDF draft combined GDP with ESG; here GDP has its own pillar and ESG sits inside Credentials, Proof of Work and Toolkit.)
